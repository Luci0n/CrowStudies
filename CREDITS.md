# Audio credits

## Mandarin syllable recordings

The bundled Mandarin pronunciation clips are derived from **GCIN Voice Data**,
recorded by Edward Der-Hua Liu (2007–2009). The corpus is available under the
GNU LGPL v3 or GNU GPL v3. CrowStudies maps its human-recorded syllables to the
course's published pinyin and tone values.

Source: https://github.com/audreyt/gcin-voice-data
